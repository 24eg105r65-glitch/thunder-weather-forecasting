Understanding Map Colors & Radar Reflectivity (dBZ)

Radar reflectivity measures precipitation density:

15 - 30 dBZ (Light to Moderate Rain) Stratiform rain or shower. Minimal lightning risk.
35 - 45 dBZ (Heavy Rain) Convective initiation. Strong updrafts.
50 - 58 dBZ (Severe Thunderstorm) Intense lightning and gusty downbursts.
60+ dBZ (Violent Hail / Supercell) High probability of large hail and microbursts.

Key Physical Indicators Explained

Schultz 2σ Lightning Jump

Sudden exponential surge in total lightning flash rate (dF/dt ≥ 2.0σ) preceding severe downbursts by 15-30 minutes.

CAPE (Updraft Energy)

Convective Available Potential Energy (>2000 J/kg) powers explosive thunderstorm updrafts.

CIN (Convective Inhibition)

Atmospheric barrier lid. Low CIN allows storms to erupt rapidly.

Cloud Top Cooling

INSAT-3D rapid cooling (≤ -5 °C / 15min) tracks vigorous updraft ascent.

Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Aerocast-AI is committed to full user privacy and data minimization. This platform operates as an open meteorological early-warning cockpit with zero monetization of personal data.

1. Zero Personal Data Tracking

We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, IP logs, or personal identifiers.

2. Ephemeral Geolocation Processing

Searched locations are processed strictly in volatile memory. Coordinates are never persisted in databases.

Emergency advisories adhere to National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) and Common Alerting Protocol (CAP v1.2) standards.
